# License and Commercialization Policy

## Selected distribution model

- **Source visibility:** source-available.
- **Third-party commercial rights:** restricted; a separate written commercial license is required.
- **Baseline source license for this branch:** PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
- **Commercial extensions, hosted services, premium connectors and enterprise deployments:** may be offered under separate commercial terms by the rights holder.
- **Brand:** separately governed; the source license does not grant trademark or endorsement rights.

PolyForm Noncommercial permits use, modification and distribution for permitted noncommercial purposes. It is **not an OSI-approved open-source license** because it excludes commercial purposes. The official license text and scope are at https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0 . It includes defined permitted uses for personal research/study and certain noncommercial organizations. Review the full text before use; commercial purpose must be assessed in context.

## Repository boundaries

### Public source-available repository

May include generic core orchestration instructions, evaluation framework, schemas, documentation, examples, non-sensitive acceptance fixtures and adapter interfaces.

### Private repositories/services

Keep production credentials, infrastructure state, customer records, billing, private incident details, unpublished security findings, private operational telemetry, proprietary deployment configurations, commercial contracts, premium connectors and any enterprise-only implementation in appropriately controlled private systems.

Do not commit secrets or customer information to either repository.

## Rights and contributors

Contributors must have authority to submit their work and agree to the repository's contribution terms. A DCO or CLA should be selected before accepting substantial external contributions. Do not assume that a GitHub handle alone proves copyright ownership or assignment.

The project's long-term governance may be transferred to a foundation, but no legal foundation ownership is asserted by this repository update. Formal incorporation, asset assignment, trademark transfer and contributor-rights policy must be completed separately before representing a foundation as legal owner.

## Historical Apache-2.0 snapshot

The repository's initial commit included an Apache-2.0 LICENSE. This branch changes the current license baseline, but does not automatically revoke rights previously granted to recipients of the earlier snapshot. Existing third-party copies and contributions must be reviewed by qualified counsel before claiming that all prior material is now restricted. The license history should remain transparent.

## Brand protection

A.S.F.R.E.E name/logo are separate from source permissions. No registration or exclusivity claim is made here. Use of the mark must not imply official endorsement, affiliation or certified compatibility. See `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Legal review required

This file records a project policy decision, not legal advice. Before merging/publishing the license change, have a qualified lawyer review: ownership chain, prior Apache grant, contributor terms, software/documentation scope, commercial-license mechanics, trademark jurisdiction and foundation transfer.
